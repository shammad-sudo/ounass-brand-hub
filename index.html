Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

A few key reminders to help everyone represent OUNASS consistently across internal materials — see the full 2025 Brand & Creative Guidelines for complete detail.
Light on dark, dark on light
Always use the black logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark backgrounds. Never place a dark logo on a dark or busy background — contrast and legibility come first.
Clear space
Keep the area around the logo free of other text or graphics. Clear zone = 2/3 the cap height of the wordmark for the English logo, 1/4 the cap height for the Arabic logo. When in doubt, give it more room, not less.
Minimum size
Never scale the master logo down below 25mm in width (or the digital equivalent) — smaller than that and the letterforms lose legibility.
Marque & O Monogram are secondary
The circular marque and O monogram are for subtle applications only — seals, stickers, favicons, gifting. They should never replace the primary wordmark or be used as the main logo in a layout.
Copper foil is an accent, not a default
The copper foil version is reserved for embellishment — foil stamping, stickers, gifting touches. Don't substitute it for the standard black/white logo in everyday print or digital use.
Don't distort or reskin the logo
Never stretch, recolor, add effects/shadows, or rotate the logo. Use the master files provided as-is.
