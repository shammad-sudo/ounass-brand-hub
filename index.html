Logo

The English wordmark is the primary logo and should be used across all brand communications. The Arabic wordmark (أناس) is used for dual-language or Arabic-only communications and has no .com variant. The O monogram and the circular marque are secondary applications — for seals, stickers, favicons and gifting, never as a primary logo.

Typography

Primary Typeface — Portrait Regular 2 (main usage)
Portrait Regular 2 — Aa Bb Cc
Portrait Medium — Aa Bb Cc
Portrait Regular 2 is our main usage weight for headings, subtitles, campaign advertising, digital marketing and print. The tagline is set in Portrait Light Caps at +75 tracking.
Portrait Medium is used for internal / copy-heavy documents only — not in hero communications.
This is a paid, licensed font (Commercial Type) — the files below are from the internal kit for authorized team use only. Licensing: commercialtype.com/catalog/portrait
Arabic Typeface — Noto Kufi Arabic Regular (main usage)
أناس — نص عربي تجريبي
Noto Kufi Arabic is our dedicated Arabic typeface in its own right — not a substitute for Portrait. Regular is the main usage weight, used on digital banners, print and e-mailers. Free & open source (SIL Open Font License).
Numericals
0123456789
Noto Serif Display is the main font for numericals; Portrait is the secondary numerical font.
Secondary / Body Copy
Nunito Sans — Aa Bb Cc
Body copy mainly uses Nunito Sans, though the exact treatment depends on the format and design — copy-heavy layouts, CRM, product copy etc. may vary by channel.
Disclaimer: sub-copy/body typography is applied at the designer's discretion per format — treat the above as general guidance, not a fixed rule.
